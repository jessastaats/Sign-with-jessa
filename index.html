Frequently asked questions

Before your appointment.

What should I bring?

Bring a current, acceptable form of identification and the complete document requiring notarization. Depending on the document, additional signers or witnesses may be required.

Should I sign the document before my appointment?

Not always. Some notarial acts require the signature to occur in the notary's presence. If you are unsure, leave the signature area blank until your appointment.

Can you come to my home, office, or another location?

Yes. Mobile notary appointments are available throughout the service area. Travel availability and any applicable travel charge can be confirmed when scheduling.

Can I have a document notarized completely online?

Many eligible documents can be handled through remote online notarization. Some documents or transactions may have additional requirements or may not qualify for online notarization.

Can a notary tell me which form I need or give legal advice?

No. A notary can perform authorized notarial acts but cannot select legal forms, prepare legal documents, or provide legal advice unless otherwise legally authorized to do so.
